The Problem

How Independent Dealers Lose
Leads On The Portals

📞

Your competitor isn't out-selling you, and they aren't beating you on price. They are just winning the platform's algorithm. Here is exactly how listings get buried behind your back:

Problem 01

The Filter Trap

A contractor filters for a 5-tonne excavator with a hydraulic hitch — or a warehouse manager searches for a 2.5t container-mast forklift. Your machine has those exact specs. But because those filter fields were left blank on upload and search terms were never updated, your machine completely vanishes from the results. To that buyer, you don't exist.

Problem 02

The Page 5 Death Spiral

Your listing went live three weeks ago — on Page 1. Today it is on Page 5. By next week, Page 7. Nobody put it there deliberately; it just sank while you were busy running the physical yard. Meanwhile, the phone call that should have been yours went to whoever showed up on Page 1 first.

Problem 03

The Competitor Snatch

A buyer clicks your listing. The photo is a blurry yard shot taken on a cloudy Tuesday, or the price is missing. Two specs are filled out instead of thirty. They click "Back" in two seconds — and land straight on your competitor's listing with clean photos and full specs. That sale was already yours. Until it wasn't.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick Answers Before You Start.

The questions every dealer asks before getting started.

Do I have to give your team my master passwords?+
No, never. Most major heavy machinery portals allow you to create a restricted "Editor" or "Staff" account. You simply invite our team as an editor. We can build, format, and optimise your listings — but we have zero access to your billing details, main account settings, or company data. Your security is 100% protected.
How do I get my raw equipment photos and specs to MachineFlo?+
It takes less than 60 seconds. The moment a new truck or machine lands in your yard, snap the photos on your phone and send them via WhatsApp or email to your assigned account manager. Our backend system automatically captures, organises, and passes them to our editing team instantly — whichever method you prefer.
How fast do listings go live after I send you the assets?+
We guarantee a turnaround time of under 24 hours. Once your raw photos and model numbers land in our system, our specialised editors clean the images, map the data sheets, cross-reference spec numbers, and push the optimised listings live across your chosen platforms — while you focus on running the yard.
What happens if I want to cancel my 90-day pilot trial?+
There are no lock-in contracts or hidden cancellation fees. We offer our 90-day introductory pilot so you can see the direct impact on your lead volume without any corporate risk. If you choose not to continue after 90 days, you can walk away with a single click.
Can you manage listings on multiple platforms simultaneously?+
Yes. Whether you list exclusively on Machines4U, or spread your inventory across Industry Search, AgTrader, Trucksales, TradeEarthmovers, HospitalityHub, or MedSearch, our editing team maps your data to match the strict algorithm requirements of every individual platform simultaneously.
