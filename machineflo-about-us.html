About Us

MachineFlo  ·  Australia-Wide  ·  sales@machineflo.com.au

We Are Not a Marketing Agency

That is the most important thing to understand about MachineFlo before anything else.

We don't run paid ads. We don't manage your social media. We don't write blog posts to boost your Google ranking. There are plenty of agencies who do all of that — and they do it well. That is not what we are.

MachineFlo is a marketplace listing management service. Our entire focus is the performance of your inventory on the portals you are already paying for. We exist in a very specific gap between the subscription you bought and the result you expected — and we close it.

Why We Exist

The equipment marketplace industry in Australia has a problem that most dealers have quietly accepted as normal.

A dealer signs up to a portal. They pay the monthly fee. They upload their stock — or get a staff member to do it when there's time. The listing goes live. And then… not much happens. The phone rings occasionally. Some enquiries come through. But never quite at the level the subscription promised, and never quite enough to make the monthly fee feel justified.

Most dealers assume the market is quiet. Or that their price needs adjusting. Or that the portal just doesn't deliver what it used to.

The machinery is fine. The price is fine. The portal is working. The listing is the problem — and nobody told you.

MachineFlo was built by someone who saw that gap repeatedly, across dealer after dealer, category after category — and decided the right answer was not another generic marketing service, but something far more specific and far more useful.

Who We Work With

We work with independent equipment dealers across every major category — earthmoving and construction, commercial transport, agricultural machinery, industrial cleaning, lifting and access equipment, material handling, hospitality, and medical equipment.

The dealers who get the most from MachineFlo tend to share a few things in common:

  • They run well-stocked yards with quality machinery but struggle to generate consistent online enquiry
  • They are paying for marketplace subscriptions that feel like they should be delivering more
  • They don't have a dedicated marketing or digital person on staff — and they don't want to hire one
  • They want to focus on running the yard and closing deals, not managing portal dashboards

If that sounds like your operation, MachineFlo was built with you in mind.

What We Actually Do

We manage the complete digital lifecycle of your equipment listings — from the moment a machine arrives in your yard to the moment a buyer picks up the phone.

That means making sure every technical specification that a buyer might filter by is correctly entered on every platform. It means making sure your listing images do justice to machinery that you have invested serious money in. It means making sure your stock doesn't quietly drift to page five while you're busy doing what you do best. And it means making sure the buyers who are actively searching for exactly what you have in your yard actually find you — not the dealer down the road with lesser stock and a better-managed listing.

We operate across Machines4U, Industry Search, AgTrader, TradeEarthmovers, TradeTrucks, HospitalityHub, and MedSearch. MachineFlo is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any of these platforms.

How We Think About Our Work

We have one standard: would a serious buyer, searching for this exact machine on this platform today, find this listing and call?

If the answer is anything other than a clear yes, there is work to do. Every filter field that is incomplete is a buyer search that returns zero results for a machine you have sitting in your yard right now. Every blurry photo is a buyer who clicks back in two seconds. Every listing that hasn't been touched this week is a listing that has moved further from the top of the page.

We treat every listing we manage as if it were our own inventory on the line. Because for our clients, it is.

We don't measure success by activity. We measure it by whether your phone rings more than it did before you engaged us.

Our Commitment to Dealers

We operate month-to-month. There are no lock-in contracts and no fine print. If MachineFlo doesn't deliver a noticeable improvement in your lead activity within 90 days, you can walk away with a single message. No invoices, no notice periods, no awkward conversations.

That is not a marketing line. It is how we structure our entire service — because we believe the only reason a dealer should stay is that the results make staying an obvious decision.

We are also completely transparent about what we are not. We are not a portal. We have no relationship with any marketplace that would influence the advice we give you. We work for the dealer — and only the dealer.
